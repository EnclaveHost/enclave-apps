Publish an IPNS name whose ed25519 key never leaves the enclave: held in attestation, not a plaintext file on disk. Records are signed in the TEE and pushed out three ways: served over /routing/v1, PUT to delegated routers, and stored on the public IPFS DHT by a hand-rolled, outbound-only libp2p stack (Noise XX, yamux, Kademlia PUT_VALUE), since rust-libp2p does not target wasip2. Byte-for-byte kubo-compatible; any node resolves the name straight from the DHT.
