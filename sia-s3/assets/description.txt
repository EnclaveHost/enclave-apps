An S3 endpoint backed by Sia, served from an attested enclave. Objects are encrypted and erasure-coded inside the enclave, then spread across independent storage hosts: any 10 of 30 shards rebuild every object, so no host can read your data and losing hosts loses nothing. Existing S3 clients work unchanged: SigV4, ranges, copy, multipart, ListObjectsV2. The key index lives in the objects' own sealed metadata, so the app keeps no local state.
